<h2></h2>
<h3 align="Center">What you'll find here ✍</h3>
<h2></h2><br>

<div align="left">
<p>
In this repository, you’ll find an applied study that investigates — using real data and statistical rigor — the financial impacts of Artificial Intelligence (AI) adoption on companies across three distinct sectors: technology, finance, and industry.

The analysis is centered on three key indicators — Revenue, Net Profit, and Return on Investment (ROI) — exploring how these metrics evolved over five years, comparing companies that adopted AI with those that did not.

Through the application of Multiple Linear Regression, the project reveals patterns, trends, and progressive effects of AI — particularly highlighting the financial sector, where gains were most significant. All data processing was automated using Python and organized within a PostgreSQL relational database, following best practices for reproducible analysis.
</p>
</div><br>

<div align="left">
   <h3>👉 Methodology</h3>
<p>
   
   > Data collection from public sources and financial reports (2020–2024)<br>
   
   > Organization into a relational database (PostgreSQL)<br>
   
   > Statistical modeling using Python (`pandas`, `statsmodels`, `matplotlib`)<br>
   
   > Interpretation of results with technical rigor and strategic insight<br>
   
   > Visualizations that enhance storytelling through data<br>
</p>
</div><br>

<div align="left">
   <h3>👉 Key Findings</h3>
<p>
   
   > The financial sector showed the `most significant impacts` of AI, especially in ROI.<br>
   
   > The technology demonstrated a `progressive maturation effect`, with results becoming visible over time.<br>
   
   > AI adoption `does not yield immediate gains`, but reveals `real value when aligned with corporate strategy`.<br>
   
   > Companies focused on innovation and digitalization outperformed others in the medium and long term.<br>
</p>
</div><br>
